### Update on App Review Guidelines: Generative AI Safety and User Disclosures (Apple)
- **Published**: Wed, 01 Apr 2026 10:00:00 PDT
- **Official Link**: [https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=ai_safety_2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=ai_safety_2026)
- **Key Topics**: AI-generated content requirements, App Review AI guidance, Safety expectations, User disclosure requirements
- **Details**: Today we are updating Guideline 1.2 and 5.1.2. Apps with generative AI features must implement robust, real-time input/output moderation and user-reporting mechanisms. Any personal data shared with third-party LLM providers must be preceded by an explicit, prominent user disclosure and consent modal. AI chatbot outputs must count towards the content age rating questionnaire.

### New Google Play Policy: AI-Generated Content Disclosures and Safeguards (Google Play)
- **Published**: Thu, 02 Apr 2026 09:00:00 PDT
- **Official Link**: [https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/04/google-play-ai-policy.html](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/04/google-play-ai-policy.html)
- **Key Topics**: Google Play AI policies, AI-generated content disclosures, User safety requirements
- **Details**: Beginning soon, Google Play will require developers of apps featuring generative AI to provide prominent in-app AI-generated content disclosures, allow users to flag/report offensive generated content, and block bad actors. Developers must prevent the generation of deepfakes, face-swaps, and non-consensual sexual content, ensuring robust user safety safeguards are active.
