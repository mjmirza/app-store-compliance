## Comprehensive Platform Policy Review

### Apple App Store AI Policies
- **AI-Generated Content Requirements**: Guidelines 1.2 and 2.3.6 mandate that applications generating text, images, or audio must reflect generative functionality in App Store Connect age ratings and disclose third-party sub-processors.
- **App Review AI Guidance**: Review teams expect apps using external LLM services (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini) to provide transparent user consent prior to transmitting personal data or user prompts.
- **Safety Expectations**: Apps must implement input/output content moderation, real-time filtering of objectionable/NSFW content, and clear in-app reporting tools for generated output.
- **User Disclosure Requirements**: Explicit consent modals are required before user data is sent to external AI services under Guideline 5.1.2(i).

### Android / Google Play AI Policies
- **Google Play AI Policies**: Google Play Developer Content Policy enforces strict compliance for generative AI applications, including Data Safety declarations and developer accountability.
- **AI-Generated Content Disclosures**: Prominent in-app user disclaimers and consent sheets must inform users before they interact with or generate AI media.
- **User Safety Requirements**: Mandatory user reporting/flagging controls must be accessible directly from AI output UI cards. Non-consensual explicit content, face-swapping, and deepfakes are strictly prohibited.
