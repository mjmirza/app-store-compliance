## Source Trust Hierarchy Verification Framework

All regulatory findings and PR proposals generated by the Regulatory Intelligence Agent must adhere to the strict five-tier Source Trust Hierarchy:

- **Priority 1 (Official Regulatory and Standardization Bodies):** European Commission, EUR-Lex, Official Journal of the European Union, ENISA, EDPB, FTC, NIST, CISA, ICO, Government publications, OFCOM, IMDA, OAIC, ANPD.
- **Priority 2 (Reputable News Agencies):** Reuters, AP (Associated Press), Bloomberg.
- **Priority 3 (Academic Publications):** Peer-reviewed journals and academic papers.
- **Priority 4 (Industry Material):** Industry blogs, vendor whitepapers, and enterprise compliance summaries.
- **Priority 5 (Social Media & AI Summaries):** LinkedIn, Reddit, Twitter/X, AI-generated summaries.

### Strict Source Verification Rule
No compliance changes or pull requests may be generated from Priority 4 or Priority 5 material unless explicitly verified and traceably corroborated by a Priority 1 official publication. Unverified Priority 4 or 5 announcements are automatically blocked from PR generation.

### 4. European Accessibility Act (EAA)

- **Announcement:** European Accessibility Act enforcement begins across all EU Member States.
- **Official Source Link:** `https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/policies/justice-and-fundamental-rights/disability/union-equality-strategy-rights-persons-disabilities-2021-2030/european-accessibility-act_en`
- **Jurisdiction:** European Union
- **Compliance Impact Level:** High

#### Detailed 7-Point Compliance Evaluation:

1. **Repository Impact:**
   - Affects UI component design guidelines, platform mechanics documentation, pre-submission checklists, and accessibility audit scripts.
   - Demands full EN 301 549 (WCAG 2.1 AA) compliance for mobile apps and web platforms.

2. **Rationale:**
   - Directive (EU) 2019/882 (EAA) mandates accessibility across consumer digital services. Mobile apps must support screen readers, font scaling, adequate color contrast, and display an accessible public statement.

3. **Identified Affected Files:**
   - `CHANGELOG.md`
   - `AGENTS.md`
   - `README.md`
   - `references/rules/performance.md`
   - `references/rules/android.md`
   - `docs/EU-REGULATORY-2026.md`
   - `docs/PLATFORM-MECHANICS-2026.md`
   - `docs/PRE-SUBMISSION-CHECKLIST.md`

4. **Suggested Implementation / Migration Steps:**
   - Verify screen-reader accessibility labels (`accessibilityLabel`, `accessibilityTraits`, `contentDescription`) across all UI elements.
   - Support Dynamic Type / system font scaling up to 200% without layout distortion or text clipping.
   - Ensure WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1 for standard text, 3:1 for large text).
   - Publish an accessible in-app Accessibility Statement linkable from metadata listings.

5. **Estimated Compliance Impact:**
   - **High Risk.** Non-compliant apps risk enforcement proceedings and store removal in EU member states.

6. **Official Citations:**
   - Priority 1: Directive (EU) 2019/882 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April 2019 on accessibility requirements for products and services.
   - Priority 1: Harmonised European Standard EN 301 549 V3.2.1 (2021-03) Chapter 11.
